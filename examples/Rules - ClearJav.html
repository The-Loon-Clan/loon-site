1. The Golden Rules (Core Principles)

Welcome to ClearJAV! We are a community dedicated to sharing Japanese Adult Video (JAV) content. Our mission is to be able to provide the highest quality JAV videos without any form of watermarking or advertising. We also aim to be able to compile the most complete archive of JAVs ever released and prevent videos from falling into obscurity. To ensure a smooth and fair environment for everyone, please read and abide by the following rules. Failure to do so may result in warnings or account removal.

  • Respect: Treat all members and staff with respect. No harassment, flaming, trolling, or discriminatory behavior (racism, sexism, etc.) will be tolerated in comments, forums, chat, or PMs.
  • Sharing is Caring: This is a community based on sharing. You are expected to contribute back by seeding what you download. Maintain required ratios and seeding times (detailed below).
  • Legality & Ethics:
    • Strictly Prohibited: Uploading or requesting any content involving minors (CSAM), non-consensual acts, bestiality, or any other material illegal in the EU. Violation leads to an immediate and permanent ban and potential reporting to authorities. ZERO TOLERANCE.
  • English is the Primary Language: All public communication (forums, comments, chat, requests) should be in English to ensure staff and members can understand. JAV titles/descriptions may include Japanese alongside English.

2. Account Security & Management

  • One Account Policy: 1 account per person, lifetime. Detected duplicates will result in all associated accounts being banned permanently.
  • IP Address Policy: Generally, only one account per IP address.
    • Seedboxes & VPN: If you use a seedbox or VPN please read this.
  • Account Security: Use a strong, unique password. Do not share your account details. You are responsible for all activity on your account. Enable 2FA if available.
  • Inactivity Rules:
    • Initial Activity: You must download or upload data within 20 days of joining, or your account may be pruned for lurking.
  • Cheating: Any attempt to falsify stats (upload, download, ratio, bonus points) using modified clients or other methods will result in an immediate, permanent ban.

3. Ratio, Seeding & Downloading Rules

  • REFER to our UPLOAD RULES as to what is allowed on the website.
  • Maintain Your Ratio: You must maintain a minimum overall ratio (Upload / Download) of 0.4. Your ratio can be viewed on your profile. Falling significantly below this may result in warnings, download restrictions, or ultimately a ban. Ratio requirements may vary based on user class/rank.
  • Hit and Run (HnR) Policy:
    • You must seed every torrent for a minimum continuous duration of 7 days (168 hours) after finishing the download. The seeding clock starts once the download completes, there's a grace period of 3 days after you disconnect (in case you need to power off your PC).
    • The required seeding time applies even if you only downloaded a portion 10%> of the torrent.
    • Accumulating 5 HnRs will result in temporary download restrictions. Check your HnR status on your profile page.
    • Each warning expires in 14 days.
    • You will receive a pre-warning 1 day before the warning, to remind you that you can still avoid it just by seeding it.
  • Torrent File Privacy: Your downloaded `.torrent` files contain your unique passkey. NEVER share these files or upload them to other sites/trackers. Doing so compromises your account and will lead to a ban.
  • Allowed Torrent Clients: Only clients from the official Allowed Client List are permitted. Using blacklisted or modified clients (including cheating clients) is forbidden.
  • Connection Limits: Typically, one connection per torrent per IP address. Do not use multiple clients on the same torrent simultaneously from the same IP.
  • Forbidden Downloading Methods:
    • Downloading/seeding directly via mobile/cellular networks is forbidden due to instability and potential IP issues.
    • Using "Cloud" torrent services (e.g., Real-Debrid, Seedr, Put.io, Offcloud, Zbigz, Torbox etc.) where you don't control the seeding environment is strictly forbidden. You must download to and seed from a machine under your direct control (PC, NAS, dedicated seedbox).

4. Upload & Duplication (Dupe) Rules

  • REFER to our TORRENT NAMING GUIDE on how to structure a torrent name.
  • Primary Identifier: The Content ID (e.g., sdde00300) is the primary identifier for a specific JAV release. Torrents with the same content ID are considered related.
  • What is NOT a Dupe (Generally Allowed to Coexist):
    • Different Resolutions (e.g., SD vs 720p vs 1080p vs 4K) of the same content ID, uploaded in the same video page (preferably upload only the max available resolution, case exception are made for 4K videos).
    • Different Sources (e.g., WEB-DL vs Blu-ray Remux vs DVD Remux) of the same release code, uploaded in the same video page.
  • What IS Likely a Dupe (Subject to Removal/Trumping):
    • Uploading the exact same file/release again.
    • Uploading a significantly lower quality version when a high-quality version already exists without justification (e.g., a 480p when a 1080p exists from the same source).
    • Minor variations of the same source/resolution/type unless providing a clear quality improvement.
  • Trumping Rules (General Guidelines - Staff may make exceptions):
    • Higher resolution generally preferred over lower, assuming comparable source/quality.
    • VP9 Codec on 4K and VR videos are preferred over H.264/AVC.
    • 5744 video bitrate for 720p releases are preferred over 4000 video bitrate on 720p releases (the 5744 video bitrate in 720p existed before covid on R18/DMM).
    • MP4 and MKV containers are preferred over WMV container, granted they are not re-encode of WMV videos and should have the same resolution as WMV (WMV were used for the first releases from R18 era).
  • Staff Discretion: Staff reserve the right to determine what constitutes a dupe based on site needs, quality standards, and member benefit. If unsure whether your upload would be a dupe, ask staff before uploading.

5. Community Interaction (Comments, Forums, Chat, DMs)

  • Torrent Comments:
    • Keep comments relevant to the specific torrent content and quality.
    • Thank the uploader! Positive feedback is encouraged.
    • NO requests for reseeds ("Seed plz!"). Use the Reseed Request button on the torrent page (after you download it for a few hours, with 0 seeders).
    • NO requests for other torrents, formats, or specific actresses/studios ("Upload XYZ!", "Need 1080p!", "More AIKA!"). Use the site's Requests system.
    • NO flaming, trolling, insulting actresses, excessive vulgarity, or disrespectful remarks. Constructive criticism regarding technical quality is okay if phrased politely.
    • NO external links.
    • NO posting personal information (yours or others).
    • NO spam, ads, referral links, or begging for bonus points/invites.
    • English language primarily for discussion.
  • Forums:
    • Search before creating new threads to avoid duplicates.
    • Post in the correct forum section.
    • Stay on topic within threads.
    • Follow general respect and conduct rules (no flaming, spam, etc.).
    • Adhere to any specific rules posted within individual forum sections.
  • Chatbox:
    • Follow general respect and conduct rules.
    • No spamming, flooding.
    • No major support requests – use forums or support tickets for complex issues.
    • Keep drama and arguments to a minimum. Report issues to staff privately.

6. Requests & Reseeding

  • Requests:
    • Use the site's official Request feature located here. Do not make requests in comments, forums, or chat.
    • Search existing torrents and active requests before creating a new one.
    • Do not request impossible things (e.g., 4K version of JAVs that were never released in 4K, content outside the site's allowed scope like non-JAV).
    • If offering a bounty (Bonus Points), ensure you have the points available. The bounty is awarded when you mark the request as filled.
    • You can request better version (for example 4K videos of a 1080p releases already uploaded) directly in the JAV page, without re-creating it.
    • Fillers: Ensure your upload accurately matches the request criteria. Upload according to all Uploading Rules (Section 4). The requester must approve your fill to release the bounty.
    • For more details check out the wiki page.
  • Reseeding:
    • If a torrent you want has 0 seeds, first start downloading (leeching) it in your client.
    • Once you are leeching, a "Request Reseed" button should appear on the torrent's page. Click this button once.
    • This action automatically sends notifications to users who previously completed the torrent.
    • Do NOT ask for reseeds manually in comments, forums, or chat. It's ineffective and spams the site. Use the button.
    • If you receive a reseed request notification (via PM or site notice) and still have the files, please help the community by seeding the torrent again for a reasonable time.

7. Bonus Points & Invites

  • Bonus Points: Earn Bonus Points primarily by seeding torrents. Points can be spent on things like Upload Credits, Creating Requests, Personal Freeleech, Invites. See the page about Bonus Points for details on earning and spending.
  • Invites:
    • Selling, trading invites is strictly forbidden and will result in a ban for both parties.
    • You are responsible for the users you invite. If they break major rules or cheat, you may also face consequences. Invite responsibly.
    • Do not beg for invites in public areas (comments, forums, chat).

8. Warnings, Disablement & Banning

  • Breaking site rules may result in consequences determined by staff, including but not limited to:
    • Informal verbal warning from staff (via PM or chat).
    • Official logged warning added to your account profile. Warnings may expire after a period of good behavior.
    • Temporary or permanent loss of privileges (downloading, uploading, commenting, chat access, invite rights).
    • Account suspension (temporary lock).
    • Permanent account ban (disabled account).
  • Severe offenses will likely result in an immediate and permanent ban without prior warning. These include:
    • Cheating or attempting to cheat ratio/stats.
    • Creating or using duplicate accounts.
    • Uploading or distributing illegal content (CSAM, non-con, etc.).
    • Selling, trading, or publicly giving away invites.
    • Sharing your unique .torrent files or passkey.
    • Severe harassment, hate speech, or threatening behavior towards members or staff.
    • Repeatedly ignoring staff instructions or official warnings.
    • Re-posting on other trackers without asking permission and crediting Personal or Internal releases, specifically Siterips.
  • Staff decisions regarding warnings and bans are final. Arguing excessively or disrespectfully with staff about decisions may lead to harsher penalties.

9. Disclaimer

  • The site staff reserve the right to modify these rules at any time without prior notice. It is your responsibility as a user to periodically review the rules and stay informed of any changes. Continued use of the site constitutes acceptance of the current rules.
  • The site staff reserves the right to disable/ban accounts and remove torrents, comments, or other content at their discretion if deemed necessary for the health, safety, legality, or smooth operation of the community.
  • These rules cover most situations, but not all. Staff will use their best judgment in situations not explicitly covered. Use common sense. If an action feels like it might violate the spirit of the rules or harm the community, don't do it.
  • This site provides access to adult material. You must be of legal age in your Country to use this site.
