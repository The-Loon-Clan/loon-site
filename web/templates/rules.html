Rules

This is a public demonstration of the loon indexer framework. It is not a private tracker and there is nothing here to earn or lose.

Accounts

  • Two demo accounts exist: alice (admin) and bob (member). The password is the username.
  • Anyone may register, but the database is reset periodically — treat every account as temporary.
  • Do not reuse a password here that you use anywhere else.

Content

  • The index holds NZB metadata harvested from public Usenet headers. It hosts no files.
  • Nothing is uploaded by members; releases come from crawling, so there is no upload moderation queue.
  • Report anything that should not be indexed through the helpdesk.

Conduct

  • The forums are moderated. Be civil.
  • Automated scraping of this demo is pointless and will be rate-limited.
