Notes

Downloaded art is written under the mounted upload volume and served from this site. Existing covers are converted in the background when the mode allows it; switching to a remote mode leaves art already stored in place rather than discarding it.

Providers differ on what they prefer. Open Library asks that public pages point at their own host, while TMDB and TVmaze make no such request — worth a look at the terms of any provider added later.
